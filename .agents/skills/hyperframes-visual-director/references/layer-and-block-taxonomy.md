# Catalog taxonomy

- `registry-block`: complete shot with its own composition and timeline; may be dominant.
- `registry-component`: reusable visual unit embedded within a scene; never a whole-film route by itself.
- `layer`: camera, focus, annotation, typography, caption, host, environment, evidence, or audio applied over a dominant visual.
- `transition`: behavior at one adjacent segment boundary.
- `motion-rule`: an animation mechanism, not a shot.
- `scene-blueprint`: composition and orchestration pattern that still needs authored content.
- `global-system`: cross-shot runtime such as CameraRig, depth/parallax, focus, environment, or mask.

For TalkCraft, preserve the original category as a browsing tag and map the seven cinematography layers to the runtime taxonomy. Hero moments may justify four to six effective layers; ordinary connective material often needs only two or three. This is a directing heuristic, never a requirement to fill all seven.

Treat a complete, self-contained product interaction as a Block. Treat target-bound annotation/focus/caption behavior as a Layer. Treat reusable choreography without complete content as a rule or blueprint.
