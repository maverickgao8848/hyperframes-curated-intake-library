# Frame policy

Every dominant visual and layer declares one policy:

- `frame-governed`: Frame controls palette, type, material, composition, and motion character. Use for teaching animation, abstract motion graphics, editorial emphasis, generic charts, and Film Credits when its exposed tokens are mapped.
- `native-evidence`: native product/media appearance is evidence and must not be reskinned. Use for WeChat, ChatGPT, browsers, operating-system UI, and faithful archive/screenshots.
- `wrapper-only`: the internal Block stays native while Frame controls the surround, border, entry/exit, or peripheral layers.
- `source-preserve`: original footage/evidence stays faithful; only safe camera, caption, annotation, evidence, or audio treatment is allowed.

Never apply Frame colors to WeChat or ChatGPT internals. A native Block may still sit inside a `wrapper-only` project treatment.
