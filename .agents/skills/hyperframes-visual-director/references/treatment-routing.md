# Dominant visual and downstream routing

Choose the dominant visual by what must carry meaning in that interval:

- `source-footage`: trust, presence, performance, or continuity depends on the original person/video.
- `external-evidence`: a source, document, screenshot, map, archive, or dataset is the evidence.
- `external-broll`: a real-world action or environment grounds the narration without itself proving a claim.
- `registry-block`: a complete, verified reusable shot already owns its composition and timeline.
- `curated-teaching`: the viewer must understand a mechanism, comparison, relationship, or sequence that needs teaching-specific design.
- `generated-visual`: the concept has no honest existing source and a generated image/video/3D asset is appropriate.

Then route:

- `direct-build` when the Block, layers, text, assets, and timing are specific enough for later construction.
- `curated-intake` when the scope is approved but teaching goal, evidence structure, Hero, beats, or Components need focused co-design.
- `media-sourcing` when an external source must be found or verified before the visual is final.
- `source-only` when the original footage remains dominant and only approved layers are needed.

Do not route every segment to teaching animation or reduce a complete UI Block to loose Components.
