# Creative defaults

These defaults apply while `hyperframes-curated-intake` is active. They guide proposals after the project-specific Frame and animated-scene scope are confirmed.

## Visual direction

- Build dense, designed frames with strong visual explanation.
- Let the selected `frame.md` govern palette, typography, material, spatial system, camera language, composition, element scale, and motion character.
- Favor visualized relationships and content-specific Heroes. Editorial, cinematic, documentary, and advertising treatments may support the message.
- Treat logos, SVGs, and Lottie as first-tier real media with equal priority.
- Use photography and video when the user supplies or explicitly requests them.
- Use a recurring prop, space, signal, or visual motif when it strengthens continuity.

## Motion and pacing

- Maintain perceptible visual activity throughout animated scenes.
- Turn reading time into progressive reveal time through type, emphasis, tracing, or meaningful ambient motion.
- Give every adjacent scene pair a designed transition. A sequence with `N` scenes carries exactly `N−1` boundary contracts.
- Classify whether adjacent scenes share an effective background color. For same-background pairs, use a transition whose luminance seam, focus plane, depth occlusion, shared object, or directional motion creates the boundary; do not depend on color contrast.
- Choose one primary transition language for most boundaries and one or two accents for topic changes, Hero reveals, or the climax. Reuse creates rhythm; accents communicate structure.
- Prefer these verified HyperFrames shader-transition Blocks when they fit the selected Frame and narrative boundary: `registry-block:cross-warp-morph`, `registry-block:domain-warp-dissolve`, `registry-block:glitch`, `registry-block:gravitational-lens`, `registry-block:ridged-burn`, `registry-block:ripple-waves`, and `registry-block:swirl-vortex`.
- Let the selected Frame determine which preferred transition fits its camera, material, contrast, and energy system.
- Keep the outgoing scene fully legible into the transition window. The shared transition performs the visual handoff while the incoming scene resolves.
- Select motion language from the approved building-block library and the chosen Frame.
- Give each scene a clear opening state, evolving middle, resolved final state, and transition-ready handoff to the next scene.

## Text

- Chinese is the primary language; English may carry concise keywords, labels, or editorial emphasis.
- Use keywords, conclusions, and short sentences. Full subtitles are opt-in.
- Favor large editorial typography, kinetic statements, typewriter sequences, and number animation.
- A quote, attribution, or central claim may become the entire Hero frame.
- Preferred text-effect vocabulary: Typewriter, Typewriter Paragraph, Text Scramble, Scramble Decode, Magnetic Pull, Binary Decode, Spotlight Reveal, Spiral In, Ink Bleed, Redacted Reveal, Progress Underline, word stagger, line reveal, and number count.
- Use [GSAPify's text-animation collection](https://gsapify.com/gsap-text-animations/) as vocabulary and visual inspiration; use the project Registry and approved HyperFrames motion rules as the executable source.
- Resolve each chosen effect through an available Registry component, approved motion rule, or a documented catalog miss before custom implementation.
- Keep the final text readable after its reveal and use deterministic, seek-safe timing.

## Registry and media

- Begin with real Registry Blocks and Components whose documented behavior serves the scene.
- Treat chart Components as evidence instruments: use them only when a quantitative relationship is the teaching goal and bind real data, title, and source note.
- Give every selected integration one visible responsibility inside the Hero or evidence structure.
- A `required` integration is a build gate. The later build completes that integration or pauses the scene with a precise blocker.
- Candidate entries remain optional until the Build Plan selects them.
- Use typewriter-key and camera-shutter SFX when their semantic event is present. Music is opt-in.

## Collaboration and autonomy

- The user confirms the Frame, animated-scene set, and the complete scene-idea review.
- After approval, the skill chooses detailed pacing, building-block recommendations, construction refinements, transitions, and evidence design.
- The skill ends after producing and validating the handoff packet. The user starts HyperFrames separately.
